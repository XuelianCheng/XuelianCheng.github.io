About me
I am currently a Research Fellow at Monash Suzhou and an Adjunct Lecturer (Assistant Professor) at Monash University Australia. Prior to this role, I completed my PhD at Monash University under the guidance of A/Prof. Zongyuan Ge , A/Prof. Mehrtash Harandi , and Prof. Tom Drummond .

My research interests include visual geometry and low-level vision tasks, i.e., Depth Estimation, Stereo Matching, 3D Reconstruction; AutoML, i.e., Neural Architecture Search (NAS); Video Interpretation, XR for Medical Education, Robotic Surgery, and Embodied AI.
