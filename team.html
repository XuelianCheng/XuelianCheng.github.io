Team
Our group works on geometry estimation, 3D reconstruction, and embodied AI for healthcare at AIM for Health (Suzhou).

Over the past two years the team has published in venues including CVPR, ECCV, AAAI, and IEEE RA-L. Our work is supported by a number of grants, including government-funded projects from the Jiangsu Department of Science and Technology and the Suzhou Industrial Park University Cooperation and Development Bureau, alongside collaborations with Southeast University and international partners.

Accepting prospective PhD students and research interns. If you're interested in working with me, please send an email including your CV and transcripts.
