Projects
EndoSurf
Neural surface reconstruction of deformable tissues from stereo endoscope videos, using neural implicit representations to recover accurate, temporally consistent 3D tissue geometry for robotic surgery.
[Paper] [Code]
HybridStereoNet
A hybrid CNN-Transformer stereo matching network for laparoscopic scenes, designed to give robust dense depth estimates under the low-texture, specular, and deforming conditions of minimally invasive surgery.
[Paper] [Code]
SLT-Net
An implicit short- and long-term motion modelling framework for video camouflaged object detection, released together with a large-scale synthetic dataset for training and benchmarking.
[Paper] [Code] [Dataset]
LEAStereo
A hierarchical neural architecture search framework for deep stereo matching that automatically discovers both the feature-extraction and matching network structures, ranking #1 on the KITTI stereo benchmark.
[Paper] [Code] [Talk]
LidarStereoNet
An unsupervised deep fusion network that combines sparse LiDAR points with stereo image pairs to produce dense, noise-robust depth estimates without requiring ground-truth depth for training.
[Paper] [Code] [Demo]
